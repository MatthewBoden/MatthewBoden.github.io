Accessibility Statement

Commitment to making this portfolio usable for as many people as possible.

Home/Accessibility

Last updated: July 27, 2026

I am committed to improving digital accessibility on matthewboden.github.io. The goal is a portfolio experience that works for people with a wide range of abilities and assistive technologies.

Conformance goal

I aim to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines help make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

Measures taken

  • Semantic HTML structure and heading hierarchy where practical
  • Skip-to-content links on legal pages
  • Descriptive labels and alt text for meaningful images where provided
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive controls
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences in parts of the experience
  • Visible focus states on interactive elements where styled

Known limitations

Some portfolio features are experimental or visually intensive (for example animated backgrounds, arcade demos, or 3D effects). These areas may present challenges for certain assistive technologies or motion sensitivity. Core content — experience, projects, contact, and resume download — is intended to remain reachable without those extras.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please tell me what page you were on and what issue you experienced. Email m.bodenstein@outlook.com. I will do my best to respond and improve.

Compatibility

The Site is designed to work with modern browsers and common assistive technologies. Older browsers or uncommon configurations may have reduced support.

Changes

This statement may be updated as the Site improves. The “Last updated” date above reflects the latest revision.
