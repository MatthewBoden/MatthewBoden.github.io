Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Matthew Bodenstein uses cookies and similar technologies on matthewboden.github.io. Read it together with the Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels. They can help a site function, remember preferences, or understand usage.

2. How this Site uses them

Strictly necessary / functional local storage

The Site’s own scripts primarily use browser local storage (not third-party advertising cookies) to remember preferences such as:

  • Audience or viewing mode preferences
  • Optional UI / debugger preferences

This data stays in your browser and is used to keep your experience consistent across visits.

Hosting and infrastructure

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages (or similar hosting). The host or network providers may process technical logs and may set cookies required for security, delivery, or infrastructure. Those practices are governed by the provider’s policies.

Analytics and advertising

This portfolio does not intentionally load third-party advertising cookies. If analytics tools are added later, this policy will be updated to describe them.

3. Managing preferences

You can control cookies and site data through your browser settings, including clearing local storage. Blocking all storage may affect optional features such as remembered preferences.

4. Third-party links

Links from this Site to LinkedIn, GitHub, project demos, Calendly-style tools, or other sites may involve cookies set by those third parties. I do not control those cookies.

5. Changes

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will reflect the latest revision.
